TOPOLOGY - THE BELT IS A BRANCHING NETWORK, not one straight line: the MAIN BELT enters at the top center and runs down to junction S1 at 38% of the screen height; there the track PHYSICALLY SPLITS into two separate belt branches like a railway switch: the LEFT branch curves down-left and feeds the BLUE gate at the bottom-left; the RIGHT branch continues down to junction S2 at 58% of the screen height, where it SPLITS again into two branches: one straight down into the GREEN gate (center bottom) and one curving down-right into the YELLOW gate (bottom-right). All three gates stand in a row at the bottom, each receiving its own branch rail. Each junction has a chunky round switch base mounted EXACTLY at the split point with a fat arrow pointer showing which branch is currently connected: S1 currently points onto the LEFT branch, S2 points straight down to GREEN.
